# Best Desk Lamps for Eye Strain

Good light matters more than most people think. We looked at **twelve** lamps and picked the ones we would actually keep on our own desks.

## What to look for

- Warm *and* cool colour modes
- A high CRI (90 or above)
  - Anything lower makes colours look flat
  - Ask the maker if it isn't listed
- No visible flicker

## Our top pick

The [Lumo Pro](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0TESTLAMP?tag=demo-20) is the lamp we recommend most. Read more on [our setup guide](/home-office-on-a-budget).

> Light your task, not the whole room.

1. Place the lamp opposite your writing hand.
2. Angle it so the bulb is out of view.

### Setup tips

Keep it simple. Move it until the glare disappears.
